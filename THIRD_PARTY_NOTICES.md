# Component sources

The design adapts the structure of Tailark Mist Hero One and Contact One. Their placeholder copy, logos, screenshots, and contacts were replaced with approved portfolio content and original diagrams.

Sources:

- https://github.com/tailark/blocks/blob/main/registry/bases/radix/mist/blocks/hero-section/one/hero-section.tsx
- https://github.com/tailark/blocks/blob/main/registry/bases/radix/mist/blocks/contact/one.tsx
- https://github.com/tailark/blocks (MIT)

Button, Input, Textarea, and Label were obtained from the official shadcn/ui New York registry and retained as reusable components.

- https://ui.shadcn.com/r/styles/new-york/button.json
- https://ui.shadcn.com/r/styles/new-york/input.json
- https://ui.shadcn.com/r/styles/new-york/textarea.json
- https://ui.shadcn.com/r/styles/new-york/label.json
- https://github.com/shadcn-ui/ui (MIT)

Third-party license texts are preserved under `licenses/`. Lucide icons, Motion, Radix UI, and local font packages retain their upstream licenses in their npm packages.
